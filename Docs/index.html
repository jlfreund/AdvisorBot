Visual Guide — Portfolio vs. Scenario Color Coding:
  • ⚙ Portfolio Settings (Slate): Household members, statements, tax jurisdiction, friendly names, and management fees — shared across all scenarios.
  • ◆ Scenario Inputs (Purple): Editable what-if assumptions (projected returns, half-lives, market corrections, spending/inflation overrides, scheduled events, and sell priority) — saved per Scenario (⌘S).
  • ★ Scenario Outcomes (Gold KPIs): Primary milestone results (Milestone Wealth, Solvency, and Tax Efficiency) recomputed from your ◆ inputs and shown on Saved Scenario cards for side-by-side comparison.

Core System Architecture

🔒 1. Local-First Confidentiality

All statements, API credentials, portfolio databases, and saved scenarios reside strictly on your local disk inside your private portfolio directory. The local web server binds exclusively to 127.0.0.1, enforces strict same-origin guards, and authenticates browser sessions automatically via restricted local session credentials.

📊 2. Evidence-Backed Empirical Baselines

Rather than guessing annual investment returns, AdvisorBot calculates holding growth rates and 7-class risk profiles from 10-year weekly Yahoo Finance total-return history, statement-reported yields to maturity (YTM), contractual fixed credited APYs, or official carrier returns.

⚡ 3. Multi-Scenario Retirement Engine

Simulate multi-decade cashflows with deterministic and stochastic models: progressive Federal & State tax brackets, IRC §86 Social Security provisional income, IRS §72 LIFO annuity rules, SECURE 2.0 RMDs (age 73 or 75), HIFO equity lot depletion, Monte Carlo solvency fans, and 1929–2025 multi-asset historical cohorts.
